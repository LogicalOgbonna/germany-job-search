Founded in Helsinki, Reaktor has offices across the globe including Amsterdam, Stockholm, Helsinki, NYC, Tokyo, and Lisbon. The company is recognized for its emphasis on work-life balance, high levels of trust and autonomy, and a collaborative engineering culture. Reaktor works with major international clients on complex digital challenges and values craftsmanship in software development.

## The Role (Job Description)

As a Frontend Engineer at Reaktor's Lisbon office, you will solve complex digital challenges at scale, share expertise with others, and work closely with Reaktor communities across global offices. Day-to-day responsibilities include:

- Writing complex software from scratch with functional JavaScript/TypeScript utilizing the latest libraries
- Managing both technical implementation and client-facing communication
- Handling the human aspects of projects and navigating challenging situations
- Creating HTML/JavaScript solutions when appropriate
- Contributing to CI pipeline setup and similar infrastructure tasks
- Collaborating with teams across Reaktor's global offices

## The Ideal Candidate

**Required:**
- 4+ years of professional web development experience
- Proficiency with React or Vue frameworks
- Knowledge of automated testing and deployment practices
- Experience with functional JavaScript/TypeScript
- Comfort mentoring both junior and senior developers
- Active participation in shaping team practices

**Preferred:**
- Consulting or client-facing background
- Understanding that non-technical solutions sometimes work best

**Tech Stack:** JavaScript, TypeScript, React or Vue, automated testing frameworks, CI/CD tools, HTML, Netlify
