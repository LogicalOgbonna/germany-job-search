# Senior Frontend Engineer

## Company Intelligence

**Teya** (formerly SaltPay) is a payment and software service provider headquartered in London, serving small and local businesses across Europe. Founded in 2019, Teya builds easy-to-use integrated tools that enable members to accept payments and boost business performance. Backed by Accel and other major investors, the company is rapidly expanding across European markets. The engineering team works on complex financial workflows including transfers, scheduled payments, account management, and Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) flows.

## The Role (Job Description)

As a Senior Frontend Engineer, you will lead the design, development, and implementation of responsive and scalable web features for the Business Account portal. Day-to-day responsibilities include:

- Building features using React, Next.js, TypeScript, and GraphQL
- Collaborating deeply with product and design teams to understand merchant user needs
- Translating complex financial workflows (transfers, scheduled payments, account management, SCA flows) into intuitive and secure user interfaces
- Building data-rich UIs handling transaction feeds, account balances, statements, and multi-currency views
- Implementing secure web flows including Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) for sensitive banking operations
- Conducting code reviews and addressing performance issues
- Supporting European market expansion with localized experiences
