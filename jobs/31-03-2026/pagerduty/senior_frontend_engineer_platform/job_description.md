# Senior Front-End Engineer - Platform

## Company Intelligence

**PagerDuty** is the global leader in digital operations management, helping teams detect and resolve critical incidents in real time. The platform is used by thousands of companies to manage their operational health. PagerDuty is publicly traded (NYSE: PD) and has established Lisbon as its second major European engineering hub. The company is known for a strong engineering culture with emphasis on reliability, scalability, and developer experience.

## Direct Application Link

https://careers.pagerduty.com/jobs/senior-front-end-engineer-platform-lisbon-portugal

## Work Model & Location

**Primarily Remote** - Lisbon, Portugal. Expected to come into the Lisbon office 1 day/month.

## The Role (Job Description)

As a Senior Front-End Engineer on the Platform team, you will lead PagerDuty in creating and implementing a new design system and component library. From day one, you will:

- Architect, code, test, and ship frontend components and libraries
- Ensure delivery of secure, highly reliable, and scalable solutions to customers
- Develop and use frontend architecture patterns that scale across the engineering organization
- Provide better user and developer experiences through shared tooling
- Mentor team members and contribute to PagerDuty's engineering culture in Lisbon

## The Ideal Candidate

**Required:**
- 6+ years of experience building and managing web apps using modern component-based frameworks
- Experience working with React
- Familiarity with Node.js and bundling tools
- Deep understanding of semantic HTML, CSS, and client-side TypeScript/JavaScript
- Experience building design systems or component libraries
- Strong mentoring and collaboration skills

**Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Node.js, modern bundling tools

## Application Strategy & Intel

Apply through the PagerDuty careers page. There is also a Senior Front-End Engineer - CSOps role in Lisbon (working on PagerDuty Status Pages) if the Platform team role is not the right fit. The Platform role is ideal for engineers passionate about design systems, shared component libraries, and developer experience. Highlight experience building reusable UI components at scale. The 1 day/month in-office requirement makes this an exceptionally flexible hybrid arrangement.
