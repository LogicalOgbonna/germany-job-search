## Job Title

## Company Intelligence
**smava** is one of Germany's largest fintech companies and the leading online credit comparison platform, making loans transparent, fair, and affordable. The company has served more than 300,000 satisfied customers. smava operates in a data-intensive distributed environment with different Node.js and TypeScript services running on AWS Lambda and Kubernetes. The company is well-established in the Berlin fintech scene with a strong engineering culture. As a comparison platform, smava handles complex frontend challenges around data visualization, form flows, and financial product presentation.
