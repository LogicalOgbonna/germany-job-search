# Senior Frontend Developer (f/m/d)

## Company Intelligence

**Company:** ZEAL Network SE
**Industry:** Online Lottery / Digital Gaming
**HQ:** Hamburg, Germany
**Stage:** Public company (Frankfurt Stock Exchange: TIMA)

ZEAL Network is Germany's largest digital lottery operator with 20+ years in the market. They operate LOTTO24 and Tipp24, and are a partner to Spain's ONCE lottery. The company has a strong digital-first culture, operating customer-facing web commerce platforms at scale. As a publicly traded company, they offer stability while maintaining a modern engineering culture with agile methodologies, code reviews, pair programming, and continuous delivery practices.
