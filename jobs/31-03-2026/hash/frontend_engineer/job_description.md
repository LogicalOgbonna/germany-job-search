# Frontend Engineer - HASH

## Job Title
Frontend Engineer

## Company Intelligence
**HASH** is building "a workspace for structured knowledge," combining knowledge capture, modeling tools, and distributed simulation of complex systems. The company is backed by notable investors and has 11-50 employees across London and Berlin offices. Founded in 2019, HASH specializes in data privacy, AI, machine learning, analytics, simulation, and agent-based modelling. The engineering culture prioritizes "standout performance in world-class environments," high energy, and ownership mentality. HASH works on complex, data-driven UIs involving knowledge graphs, world-modeling, and sophisticated visualizations, making this an intellectually stimulating frontend role.

## The Role (Job Description)
- Design, build, and ship production-ready React/TypeScript components and app-wide features for complex data-driven UIs
- Work with mathematical experts to convert world-modeling theory into software requirements
- Profile and optimize rendering performance for complex interactions and visualizations
- Build process/model-oriented UIs (BPMN, UML, systems-dynamics models, graphs)
- Contribute to open-source projects and maintain well-documented, semantically versioned libraries
- Take technical leadership including mediating discussions around standards and implementation approaches
- Take accountability for feature delivery

## The Ideal Candidate
**Required:**
- Expertise in React, TypeScript, modern HTML & CSS
- Proven track record architecting and delivering rich web apps involving complex user interactions
- Technical leadership experience
- Understanding of WCAG 2.2 and pragmatic accessibility techniques
- Familiarity with process/model-oriented UIs (BPMN, UML, systems-dynamics models, graphs)
- Public record of maintaining well-documented, semantically versioned libraries (ideally open-source)

**Nice-to-Have:**
- Experience with WebGL, Canvas, or complex rendering/visualization
- Data visualization expertise
- Experience with knowledge graphs or simulation tools

**Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, complex data visualizations
