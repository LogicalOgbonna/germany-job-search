# Senior Frontend Engineer

## Company Intelligence

**ComplyAdvantage** is the financial industry's leading source of AI-driven financial crime risk data and detection technology. Their mission is to neutralise the risk of money laundering, terrorist financing, corruption, and other financial crimes. More than 1,000 companies rely on ComplyAdvantage to understand the risk of who they're doing business with through the world's only global, real-time database of people and companies. Backed by Goldman Sachs, Ontario Teachers, Index Ventures, and Balderton Capital. Five global hubs in New York, London, Singapore, Lisbon, and Cluj-Napoca.

## Work Model & Location

**Hybrid** - Lisbon, Portugal. Two days per week in the office to foster collaboration.

## The Role (Job Description)

As a Senior Frontend Engineer, you will:

- Develop new features for ComplyAdvantage's SaaS-based anti-money laundering solutions
- Help make right technical and architectural decisions
- Take a lead in discussions on frontend architecture
- Write tests and ensure code quality across the frontend codebase
- Coach and mentor team members while sharing best practices
- Collaborate with backend software engineers on frontend applications

## The Ideal Candidate

**Required:**
- 5+ years experience writing production-grade TypeScript with React or similar framework-based applications
- Awareness of a range of languages and paradigms
- Ability to coach and mentor junior engineers
- Strong communication and collaboration skills

**Tech Stack:**
- **Frontend:** TypeScript, ES6+, React
- **Backend (context):** Kotlin, Python
- **Infrastructure:** AWS, GCP
- **Database:** Postgres, Yugabyte
- **Communication:** Kafka (event-sourced model), gRPC
- **Observability:** Grafana Cloud
- **Deployment:** ArgoCD
