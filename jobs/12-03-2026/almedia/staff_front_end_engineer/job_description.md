## Work Model & Location
**On-site** — Berlin, Germany. Modern, centrally located office designed to foster collaboration.

## The Role (Job Description)
- Develop clean, efficient, and scalable code using React and TypeScript
- Create data-driven products by integrating external services for A/B testing and feature flagging
- Deliver high-quality testable code at a high pace
- Collaborate closely with the CTO, backend teams, and Product team to bring the product vision to life
- Full-stack contributions are a plus (NestJS backend)

## The Ideal Candidate
- **Experience:** 7+ years of professional experience
- **Core Stack:** React, TypeScript, NestJS
- **Skills:** Functional understanding of JS backends (e.g., NestJS) to facilitate seamless collaboration and end-to-end development
- **Nice-to-Have:** Experience writing React Native applications
- **Soft Skills:** Great eye for design, self-starter with strong ownership mindset, creative and solution-driven, excellent communication skills
- **Language:** Very good English skills (written and spoken) — mandatory
