## Job Title

The company serves 900+ million users annually and has offices in Berlin, Prague, Melbourne, Brazil, Bangalore, and London. This role is for their new Ads Product team, building the next generation of native travel advertising — a premium product suite connecting top tourism brands with tens of millions of global travelers. This is a greenfield opportunity to build a brand-new monetisation engine from scratch.
