# Senior Fullstack Engineer - TypeScript & NextJS

## Job Title
Senior Fullstack Engineer - TypeScript & NextJS

## Company Intelligence
**Climatiq** is a fast-growing Berlin-based climate tech startup embedding carbon intelligence into business decisions worldwide. Their platform combines the world's leading emissions database with sophisticated AI and calculation tools, used by tens of thousands of sustainability leaders worldwide. Climatiq is a certified B Corp, backed by renowned international VCs. The company is remote-first with their HQ in Berlin and team distributed across Europe. Their tech stack is notably modern and differentiated, using Rust for their distributed Edge API alongside TypeScript/Next.js for frontend applications.

## The Role (Job Description)
- Design, build, and maintain modern frontend features across core web applications and interfaces
- Collaborate closely with product, design, and backend engineering to deliver intuitive, performant user experiences
- Take ownership of key frontend components and contribute to architectural decisions
- Develop and iterate on full-stack features using TypeScript across the stack and Next.js on the frontend
- Shape the look, feel, and functionality of data-intensive tools that empower users to understand and reduce their emissions

## The Ideal Candidate
- **Experience:** 3+ years of production experience with TypeScript and Next.js
- **Core Stack:** TypeScript, Next.js, PostgreSQL
- **Skills:** Building and maintaining full-featured web applications, RESTful API integration, state management, component-based UI architecture, strong working knowledge of PostgreSQL or similar databases
- **Nice-to-Have:** Familiarity with Rust and/or AWS infrastructure
- **Soft Skills:** Structured, outcome-oriented mindset with strong problem-solving and communication skills, self-driven and comfortable working autonomously in a distributed team setting
