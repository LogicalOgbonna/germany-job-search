# Software Engineer - Full Stack

## Company Intelligence

**Cello** is an AI-powered referral and revenue growth infrastructure platform for modern SaaS businesses. Their product enables companies to build, automate, and scale user referral programs, partner networks, and affiliate programs directly embedded within their products. Founded by a team with backgrounds at Twilio, Wise, and Skype, Cello has raised $8.3M in funding from top-tier investors.

**Traction:** 15+ new customers per month, 10M+ monthly end users across their multi-tenant infrastructure processing millions of events. Notable customers include Typeform, Miro, Descript, Veed, tl;dv, Smoobu, Ideogram, Riverside, and GitKraken.

**Engineering culture:** Small, developer-first team where engineers have real ownership and shape how things are built. They heavily adopt AI tools for productivity. The platform is technically interesting: attribution models, recommendation engines, embeddable widgets running in customer environments, and high-throughput event processing. Not typical CRUD work. Remote-first with regular in-person gatherings.

**Compliance:** GDPR and CCPA compliant, EU-based servers, SOC-II certification in progress.

## The Role (Job Description)

Work across the full stack on Cello's referral infrastructure platform, touching everything from frontend widgets to backend infrastructure to data pipelines. Core areas:

- Build features and fix bugs with gradual ownership increases over time
- Work on APIs, attribution models, and recommendation engines
- Develop code that runs in customer environments you don't control (embeddable widgets)
- Build and maintain the campaign management portal
- Work alongside senior engineers who provide guidance and mentorship
- Leverage AI tools heavily to move fast and learn faster
- Contribute to multi-tenant infrastructure handling millions of events monthly serving 10M+ end users
