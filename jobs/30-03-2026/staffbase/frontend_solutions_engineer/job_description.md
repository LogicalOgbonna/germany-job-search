## The Role (Job Description)

This is a customer-facing frontend engineering role that bridges technical implementation with design consulting for enterprise customers. Day-to-day responsibilities include:

- Serve as a technical and design advisor to enterprise customers
- Translate business requirements into UI/UX and frontend customization solutions
- Create mockups, prototypes, and design concepts for customer implementations
- Design and build frontend implementations using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React
- Provide expertise on theming, custom CSS, and component architectures within the Staffbase platform
- Lead customer workshops and implementation roadmaps
- Identify technical risks and scalability concerns in customer solutions
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including designers, engineers, and product managers
- Gather structured customer feedback to influence the product roadmap
