## The Role (Job Description)
DoorDash seeks a frontend-focused engineer for its SevenRooms hospitality technology platform. The role involves:
- Designing, developing, testing, and maintaining customer-facing features
- Deploying highly performant, mission-critical systems
- Serving as technical owner throughout the feature lifecycle (conception to deployment)
- Collaborating with cross-functional teams (engineers, product managers, designers)
- Managing project priorities and code quality
- Participating in occasional on-call responsibilities
- Owning features from conception through deployment
