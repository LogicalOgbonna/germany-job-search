# Langdock Application Form Answers

## Please explain in three to five sentences: Why do you want to join Langdock?

Langdock's growth from EUR 1M to EUR 15M+ ARR in one year tells me the model-agnostic AI abstraction layer you have built genuinely solves a real enterprise problem, not just a demo. The engineering challenges behind that, prompt caching across providers, resumable streaming infrastructure, and agent orchestration with human-in-the-loop workflows, are exactly the problems I have been solving in production with my own multi-agent systems. I want to work somewhere the AI infrastructure is the product, not a bolt-on feature, and Langdock is one of the few companies where the fullstack TypeScript work and the AI systems work are the same thing. The small, intense Berlin team shipping at this velocity is the environment where I do my best work.

## Please explain what part of our actual product or any feature excites you most to work on and why?

The agent and workflow automation layer excites me most. I recently built a multi-agent AI system with 5 specialized agents using Mastra, including three-stage intent classification, Redis-coordinated execution, and a hybrid RAG pipeline over 708K+ vector embeddings with pgvector, BM25, and Cohere reranking. The hard problems in agentic workflows, making agents pause reliably for human input, chaining actions across integrations without losing state, and building resumable streaming so users can disconnect mid-response and pick up exactly where they left off, are the problems I find most interesting in software engineering right now. Langdock is solving these at enterprise scale across multiple LLM providers, which adds a layer of complexity (provider-specific quirks, failover, caching) that makes the work even more compelling.

## What was the most interesting project that you've worked on?

OurNigeria (ournigeria.ng), an AI-powered platform for Nigerian budget analysis and corruption tracking. I built the entire system end-to-end: an Nx monorepo managing 6 applications (NestJS API, Next.js citizen web app, admin dashboard, document ingestion pipeline, marketing site, and a Remotion video service). The core is a multi-agent AI system with 5 specialized agents and a hybrid RAG pipeline that processes 700+ government budget documents (708K+ vector embeddings across 37 states), letting citizens query government spending in plain English or Pidgin. The technical depth was immense: SSE streaming with keepalive heartbeats, pgvector + BM25 search with Reciprocal Rank Fusion and Cohere reranking, Prisma with custom migration tooling for pgvector, Docker Compose production deployment with health checks and memory limits, and Langfuse for LLM observability. What makes it the most interesting is that it is not a toy, real citizens use it to hold their government accountable, and every architectural decision (from embedding dimensions to streaming heartbeat intervals) had to work reliably in production.
