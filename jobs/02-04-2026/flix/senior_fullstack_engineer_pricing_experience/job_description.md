# Senior Fullstack Engineer, Pricing Experience

## Company Intelligence

**Flix** (formerly FlixBus/FlixTrain) is Europe's largest long-distance mobility provider, operating bus and train services across 40+ countries. Headquartered in Munich with a major tech hub in Berlin, Flix has grown from a startup to a global brand with over 5,000 employees. The Pricing Experience team is responsible for building dynamic pricing systems that power how millions of customers see and interact with fares. The engineering culture emphasizes technical excellence, domain-driven design, and modern architecture patterns.

## The Role (Job Description)

As a Senior Fullstack Engineer on the Pricing Experience team, you will contribute to developing dynamic pricing systems using TypeScript, React, and Node.js. Key responsibilities include:

- Building and maintaining frontend and backend services for pricing and fare display
- Developing dynamic pricing UIs that serve millions of customers
- Making architectural decisions and driving technical excellence through lateral leadership
- Collaborating with cross-functional teams on pricing strategy implementation
- Contributing to the migration and evolution of core products
- Working with modern architecture patterns (DDD, Hexagonal Architecture, Ports & Adapters)

## The Ideal Candidate

- 5+ years of experience in software engineering, with at least 3+ years of fullstack development
- Expertise in React and backend frameworks like NestJS or Express
- Advanced proficiency in TypeScript, with sophisticated type-safety techniques
- Experience with Domain-Driven Design, Hexagonal Architecture, Ports & Adapters, and SOLID principles
- Proficient in MySQL/PostgreSQL, key-value stores
- Experience with DevOps tools: Docker, Terraform, CI/CD pipelines
- Strong focus on architecture, system design, and code quality

## Tech Stack

TypeScript, React, NestJS/Express, Node.js, MySQL/PostgreSQL, Docker, Terraform, CI/CD, AWS/Azure, Kafka
