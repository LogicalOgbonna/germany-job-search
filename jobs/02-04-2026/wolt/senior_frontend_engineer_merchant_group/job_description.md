# Senior Frontend Engineer, Merchant Group

## Company Intelligence

**Wolt** is a Finnish technology company (part of DoorDash since 2022) operating a delivery platform across 23+ countries. The Merchant Group is responsible for building tools that help merchants (restaurants, shops, venues) operate efficiently on the platform. The group consists of four teams: Merchant App (iPad operations tool used by 50,000+ venues), Merchant Tools (onboarding and lifecycle management), Restaurant Menu (menu editor), and Merchant Integrations (automation tools). Teams are highly autonomous, handling the entire development lifecycle themselves.

## The Role (Job Description)

You will join one of four teams within the Merchant Group, improving the Wolt experience by helping merchants serve more customers faster while enabling them to focus on what matters. Key responsibilities include:

- Building and maintaining merchant-facing applications using React + TypeScript
- Handling the full development process: business logic, testing, database & data streaming, CI/CD, analytics, and cloud infrastructure
- Working autonomously within a cross-functional team, with support from central teams when needed
- Contributing to architecture decisions and technical direction
- Collaborating closely with product managers, designers, and backend engineers

## The Ideal Candidate

- Proficiency in React + TypeScript with several years of professional experience
- Several years of experience building high-quality software, including working with apps, APIs, and integrations, handling the whole development cycle all the way to production
- Strong communication and collaboration skills — ability to express opinions and work together with the team
- Ownership mindset and ability to work autonomously
- Note: Seniority at Wolt is measured by skills, not years on your CV

## Tech Stack

React, TypeScript, Kotlin, Python, MongoDB, Kafka, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Terraform, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes
