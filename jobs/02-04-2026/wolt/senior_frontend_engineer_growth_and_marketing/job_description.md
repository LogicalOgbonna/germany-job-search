# Senior Frontend Engineer, Growth & Marketing

## Company Intelligence

**Wolt** is a Finnish technology company (part of DoorDash since 2022) that operates a delivery platform for food, groceries, and retail products across 23+ countries. Wolt has grown into one of Europe's leading delivery platforms, with major tech hubs in Helsinki, Berlin, and Stockholm. The engineering culture is known for high autonomy, fast iteration, and a strong emphasis on testing and experimentation. The Growth & Marketing team specifically focuses on customer acquisition and engagement through consumer-facing applications.

## The Role (Job Description)

The Growth & Marketing team works on making changes to consumer-facing applications (iOS, Android, and Web) and building tools for local and central marketing teams. Development within the team moves at a fast pace, is very iterative, involves a lot of testing out new things, and finding new ways to engage customers. Day-to-day responsibilities include:

- Building high-quality React + TypeScript applications for consumer-facing web experiences
- Developing tools for marketing teams to manage campaigns and customer engagement
- Running growth experiments and A/B tests to optimize user acquisition and retention
- Collaborating with designers and product managers to deliver delightful user experiences
- Ensuring code is maintainable, well-tested, and works in a multi-team codebase
- Contributing to the full development process (business logic, testing, CI/CD, analytics, cloud)

## The Ideal Candidate

- Proficiency in building high-quality React apps in TypeScript with several years of professional experience
- Several years of experience building high-quality software across the full development cycle, all the way to production
- Strong visual quality and UX sensibility — ability to build delightful experiences
- Good communication and collaboration skills with cross-functional teams
- Experience working in a codebase with contributors from many different teams
- Bonus: Previous experience with growth hacking or experimentation

## Tech Stack

React, TypeScript, Python, Kotlin, Scala, MongoDB, Kafka, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Terraform, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes
