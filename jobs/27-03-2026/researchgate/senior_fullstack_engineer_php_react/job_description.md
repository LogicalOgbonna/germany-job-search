# Senior Fullstack Engineer PHP/React (m/f/d)

## Job Title
Senior Fullstack Engineer PHP/React (m/f/d)

## Company Intelligence
**ResearchGate** is the largest professional network for scientists and researchers, with over 25 million members from 193+ countries. Founded in 2008 and headquartered in Berlin, the platform connects researchers to collaborate and advance their research. ResearchGate operates as a remote-first organization with a central office in Berlin, fostering a culture of autonomy, continuous learning, and cross-functional collaboration. The company builds researcher-facing products at scale on a cloud-native (GCP) infrastructure.

## The Role (Job Description)
- Design, develop, and deploy full-stack components for researcher-facing products
