# The Role (Job Description)
- Central role in advancing plancraft’s AI product, PORTA (a voice-first digital receptionist).
- Join the dedicated PORTA team (1 PM, 1 UX Designer, 4 Engineers) to take ownership of evolving AI Frontdesk Capabilities.
- Lead the development and continuous improvement of AI-driven features, leveraging LLMs and voice technologies across multiple communication channels.
- Design, build, and operate scalable systems on GCP, using React in the frontend and serverless Node.js/Express services in the backend (all in TypeScript).
- Shape and evolve AI capabilities by iterating on prompts, evaluations, and system behavior.
- Drive architectural decisions and set engineering standards for long-term scalability, maintainability, and performance.
- Take end-to-end ownership of features from scoping and technical design to delivery, monitoring, and iteration.
- Improve developer experience by advancing tooling, workflows, and codebase quality (e.g., refactoring, AI-assisted development, testing practices).
- Mentor and support engineers through hands-on collaboration, code reviews, and knowledge sharing (Individual Contributor role, no formal people management).
- Collaborate closely with Product and Design.

# The Ideal Candidate
- **Experience:** 5+ years of software engineering experience with a proven track record of technical leadership.
- **Tech Stack:** Extensive TypeScript experience with advanced patterns and best practices. Deep cloud experience (GCP, AWS, or Azure) with infrastructure design capabilities.
- **AI Expertise:** Advanced AI expertise and market awareness (real-time voice, multimodality, LLM integration, and evaluations). Expert-level familiarity with modern, AI-enhanced engineering tools and workflows (Cursor, Claude Code, GitHub Copilot).
- **Skills:** Strong software engineering background with system design expertise. Extensive experience teaching, advocating for technology choices, and mentoring.
- **Soft Skills:** Strong communication skills and proven ability to drive alignment with business & tech stakeholders. Experience leading technical initiatives and driving organizational change.
- **Languages:** English at C1 level is required. German is a plus, but not a must.
- **Nice-to-haves:** Experience building/shipping LLM-powered features/agents to production; experience ensuring quality/reliability in non-deterministic AI systems; advanced Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Pulumi); proven experience scaling SaaS/B2B products; experience with Firebase and complex GCP services (Cloud Functions, Cloud Run, BigQuery); PO/PM experience with technical teams; previous Staff Engineer or Tech Lead role.
