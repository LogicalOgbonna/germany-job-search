# (Senior) AI Engineer, HR Applications (m/w/d), Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg

## Company Intelligence

Tesla, Inc. is a publicly traded ($TSLA, NASDAQ) global manufacturer of electric vehicles, energy storage, and solar products, headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2003, Tesla operates one of the most ambitious vertically integrated engineering organisations in the world, spanning automotive, energy, robotics (Optimus), AI/autonomy (FSD, Dojo), and manufacturing.

**Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg** (Grünheide, Brandenburg) is Tesla's European manufacturing and engineering hub, producing the Model Y and cell components. The site employs roughly 11,000+ people and continues to scale production capacity. Internal engineering culture is famously fast-paced, owner-mentality oriented, low on process, and unusually high on autonomy: small teams ship production systems used by tens of thousands of employees with very short feedback loops.

This particular role sits inside the **HR Engineering / People Tech** function, an internal product team that builds bespoke software to replace or augment off-the-shelf HRIS tooling. It is one of the more recent investments at Tesla in applied LLM/agentic systems for internal operations, alongside similar efforts in finance and supply chain. Engineering here resembles a startup embedded inside a manufacturing giant: full-stack ownership, no PM hand-offs, and direct collaboration with HR leadership.

## The Role (Job Description)

Build AI-powered production products that change how HR operates at Tesla. End-to-end ownership: architect, ship, and maintain systems used by thousands of Tesla employees globally. Day-to-day responsibilities:

- Partner directly with HR leadership, HR business partners, and cross-functional stakeholders to identify high-impact opportunities and convert them into shipped software.
- Architect, develop, deploy, and maintain LLM-powered production systems with measurable business impact (cycle-time reductions, cost savings, employee experience metrics).
- Drive technical decisions on AI/ML architecture, model selection (open vs closed), RAG vs fine-tune vs prompt-only, and evaluation frameworks.
- Apply rigorous engineering practices: version control, automated testing, CI/CD pipelines, containerised deployments, observability.
- Mentor junior engineers and interns; raise the bar for AI/ML development practices across the team.
- Translate between technical implementation and non-technical HR stakeholders; ruthlessly prioritise what is worth building.

## The Ideal Candidate

**Required:**
- 3+ years of software engineering experience with a track record of shipping production applications (solo and in teams).
- Deep, hands-on experience building production systems with LLMs: RAG pipelines, agentic frameworks, evaluation, iteration on real users. Must have shipped AI products independently, not just prototyped.
