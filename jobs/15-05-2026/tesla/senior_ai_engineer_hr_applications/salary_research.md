## Notes

- Location: Grünheide (Mark), Brandenburg, Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg.
- Role uses "(Senior)" parenthetical, implying mid-to-senior bar, likely Tesla P2 to P3 internal level.
- Tesla EU compensation includes base + Tesla shares (RSUs) and/or annual bonus, plus 30 vacation days, occupational pension, life/disability insurance, free EV charging.
- AI Engineer specifically is not broken out on Levels.fyi for Berlin; SWE bands are the closest proxy. Specialised AI roles at Tesla in the US typically sit at the top of band.
- Senior Software Engineer Glassdoor estimate (GBP, region-wide): GBP 57.6K to GBP 102K (25th to 75th percentile), median GBP 75K. Roughly EUR 67K to EUR 119K at current rates.

## Negotiation Targets

- Push higher (EUR 115K+) if multi-provider LLM, evals, and infra-from-scratch story lands. Tesla Berlin pays meaningfully below SF/Palo Alto bands; do not anchor against US Tesla numbers.
