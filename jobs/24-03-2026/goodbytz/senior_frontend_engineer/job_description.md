# Senior Frontend Engineer (all genders welcome)

## Job Title
Senior Frontend Engineer (all genders welcome)

## Company Intelligence
**goodBytz** is a Hamburg-based food robotics startup building autonomous kitchen systems for the food service industry. The company develops robotic kitchens that can prepare meals autonomously, serving both B2B clients (restaurants, canteens) and B2C ordering platforms. goodBytz operates at the intersection of robotics, IoT, and consumer-facing web platforms. The engineering culture emphasizes shipping fast while maintaining quality, flat hierarchies, and a first-name-basis team culture. The company has received significant backing and is growing its engineering team.

## The Role (Job Description)
You will translate operational concepts into intuitive interfaces for both B2B and B2C platforms powering robotic kitchens. Day-to-day responsibilities include:

- Developing frontend applications for B2B and B2C platforms (ordering solutions, cloud systems)
- Integrating REST APIs and live communication protocols (SSE, WebSockets, MQTT) with backend teams
- Collaborating with UI/UX and product teams on user-centric design solutions
- Building responsive, high-performance interfaces using modern frameworks
- Maintaining code quality through clean architecture and testing
- Leading frontend projects and mentoring junior developers
- Monitoring performance and user analytics for continuous optimization

## The Ideal Candidate

**Required Technical Skills:**
- 7+ years professional frontend development experience (senior level)
- Expertise with React, AngularJS, or comparable UI frameworks
- REST APIs and live protocol integration (SSE, WebSockets, MQTT)
- Cross-functional problem-solving abilities
- Junior developer mentoring experience
- English fluency (C1+)

**Education:** Computer Science/Software Engineering degree or equivalent

**Soft Skills:**
- High ownership and bias for shipping fast while maintaining quality

**Tech Stack:** React, REST APIs, SSE, WebSockets, MQTT
