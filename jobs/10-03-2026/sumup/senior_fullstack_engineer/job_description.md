# (Senior) Fullstack Engineer

## Company Intelligence

**SumUp** is a leading global fintech company empowering small businesses with payment solutions and financial tools across 36 markets. SumUp is one of Europe's most valuable fintech companies, known for its card readers, POS systems, and business accounts. The company has a strong engineering culture and is headquartered in multiple European cities with Berlin being a major hub. The Engineering / Platform Tribe (Field Sales Engineering Team) builds internal tools and systems that power SumUp's sales operations.

## Work Model & Location

- **Model:** Office-first (Berlin-based)
- **Location:** Berlin, Germany

## The Role (Job Description)

- Design and maintain services for dependable, highly available production systems following microservice architecture
- Develop scalable, high-quality code adhering to platform standards
- Collaborate with stakeholders to identify pain points and gather feature requirements
- Partner with Sales, Data, and Software Engineers across Platform and Operations teams

## The Ideal Candidate

### Must-Have
- 5+ years TypeScript programming experience
- Proficiency with fullstack frameworks (Next.js, Remix, Nuxt, or Meteor)
- RESTful API development and consumption in microservice environments
- SQL and event-driven architecture experience (PostgreSQL and Kafka)
- Understanding of system architecture, software design principles, data modeling, and API design
- Collaborative mindset with openness to feedback
- Strong ownership mentality

### Tech Stack
TypeScript, Next.js/Remix/Nuxt, PostgreSQL, Kafka, RESTful APIs, Microservices
