# Staff Front End Engineer - Grafana K6

## Job Title
Staff Front End Engineer - Grafana K6

## Company Intelligence
**Company:** Grafana Labs
Grafana Labs is the company behind Grafana, the leading open-source platform for observability and monitoring. Their products include Grafana, Loki, Tempo, Mimir, and k6 (performance testing). They have a strong open-source culture, a fully remote global workforce, and are one of the most respected names in developer tooling and infrastructure. They are well-funded and growing rapidly with transparent compensation.

## The Role (Job Description)
As a Staff Front End Engineer on the Grafana K6 team, you will build and shape the frontend experiences for k6, Grafana's performance testing tool. You will work on complex frontend applications using TypeScript and React, contributing to both the product and technical direction of the team.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain complex frontend applications for k6
- Drive technical excellence and architectural decisions
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams on product development
- Contribute to Grafana's open-source ecosystem

## The Ideal Candidate
### Required
- At least a couple of years as a Senior Frontend Engineer
- Excellent TypeScript skills
- Solid React.js experience (2+ years day-to-day)
- Experience building complex frontend applications

### Tech Stack
- TypeScript, React.js

## Benefits
- 30 days annual leave + 3 shutdown days
- RSUs (Restricted Stock Units)
- In-person onboarding experience
- Fully remote global culture

## Application Strategy & Intel
Grafana Labs is open-source-focused. Contributing to k6 or Grafana open-source projects before applying would make you stand out. Highlight any experience with developer tooling, performance testing UIs, and complex data visualization. The company values technical depth and open-source contributions.
