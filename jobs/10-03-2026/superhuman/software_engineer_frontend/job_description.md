# Software Engineer, Front-End

## Company Intelligence

**Superhuman** (formerly Grammarly) is an AI productivity platform serving over 40 million users across 50,000 organizations and 3,000 educational institutions. The suite includes Grammarly (writing assistance), Coda (collaborative workspaces), Mail (inbox management), and Go (proactive AI assistant). The company has undergone a major rebrand/merger, combining Grammarly's writing AI with broader productivity tools. Superhuman operates from 7 global hubs and is a well-funded, publicly recognized leader in AI-assisted productivity. Their Berlin hub is their newest European office, signaling significant investment in the European engineering talent market.
