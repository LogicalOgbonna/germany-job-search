# Senior Full Stack Developer (TypeScript)

## Job Title
Senior Full Stack Developer (TypeScript)

## Company Intelligence
**Cosuno** is a Berlin-based construction tech (ConTech) startup building a digital platform for subcontractor management in the construction industry. The platform leverages AI to streamline procurement processes, including BoQ (Bill of Quantities) editing agents and bidder recommendation systems. Cosuno operates as a well-funded, stable organization with a monorepo architecture and a strong emphasis on code quality (strict typing, linting, Cypress E2E testing). The company culture is engineering-driven with a pragmatic, ownership-oriented mindset.

## The Role (Job Description)
You will work as a senior full-stack developer with significant autonomy and end-to-end feature ownership. Day-to-day responsibilities include:

- Designing and implementing AI-powered features, including BoQ editing agents and bidder recommendations
- Owning features end-to-end from concept through deployment
- Collaborating with product and design teams on user experience and technical direction
- Architecting modular packages and services within a monorepo structure
- Ensuring code quality through strict typing, linting, and E2E testing (Cypress)
