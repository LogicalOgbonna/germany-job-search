Finanzcheck is part of the smava/Finanzcheck group, backed by Verdane (growth equity). The company operates at scale with high-traffic B2C conversion-focused frontends.

## The Role (Job Description)
You will join the **Website and Registration Route** team, responsible for welcoming every website visitor and making the entry to Finanzcheck's loan comparison service clear, simple, and convenient. Day-to-day responsibilities include:

- Developing React frontends in a data-intensive distributed environment connected to NodeJS Serverless and Golang backend services running on AWS Lambda and Kubernetes
- Conceptualizing and building new functionality as well as improving existing systems and services
- Building and maintaining A/B testing infrastructure and running continuous optimization tests
- Collaborating closely with Product Owners and UX Designers
- Full DevOps responsibility under a "you build it, you run it, you own it" philosophy
- Participating in team decision-making on tools, timelines, and technical approaches

## The Ideal Candidate

**Required Technical Skills:**
- Professional experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript/TypeScript, React, and NextJS (static page generation)
- Experience setting up A/B testing infrastructure and running continuous optimization tests
- Experience building frontend applications that integrate with complex backend APIs and services
- Familiarity with AWS services (CloudFront, Lambda, S3)
- Proven experience building customer journeys and high-traffic frontends in conversion-focused B2C environments
- Solid understanding of accessibility best practices and performance optimization
- Experience with analytics/customer data platforms (Segment, Amplitude)

**Nice to Have:**

**Soft Skills:**
- Strong communication and collaborative mindset
- Proactive working style with ownership mentality

**Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, NextJS, NodeJS, Golang, AWS (Lambda, CloudFront, S3), Kubernetes
