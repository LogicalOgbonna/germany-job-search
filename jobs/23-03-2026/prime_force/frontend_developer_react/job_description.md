# Frontend Developer / Enthusiast

## Job Title
Frontend Developer / Enthusiast

## Company Intelligence
**Prime Force Group** is an international IT consulting and development company operating across Berlin, Vienna, and Salzburg. The company focuses on delivering frontend development projects for enterprise clients in an international environment. Prime Force works in agile teams and emphasizes professional development, certifications, and career flexibility — engineers can pivot between developer, consultant, and trainer roles. The company is mid-sized with a collaborative, team-oriented culture and offers both office and remote work options.

## Direct Application Link
https://prime-force.com/jobs/listing/frontend-developer-enthusiast/351

## Work Model & Location
Hybrid — Berlin, Vienna & Salzburg (work in offices and remotely)

## The Role (Job Description)
You will work on exciting frontend development projects in an international environment, using modern frameworks and technologies. The role involves:

- Successfully realizing client-facing projects using modern frontend frameworks
- Working in agile team environments with a strong focus on quality
- Collaborating with international teams and clients
- Contributing to frontend architecture and technology decisions

## The Ideal Candidate

**Required Technical Skills:**
- Passion and experience with modern frontend frameworks: Vue JS, React, Angular, Bootstrap, Nuxt, Flutter
- Strong understanding of frontend development principles

**Soft Skills:**
- Strong customer orientation and excellent communication abilities
- Collaborative team player in agile environments
- Service-minded with reliability and diligence
- Professional demeanor with clients and colleagues
- Fluent in German and English (both required)

- **Application Method:** Apply via the Prime Force website at the link above (upload resume via online form)
- **Response Time:** The company commits to responding "promptly via email"
- **Documents Required:** Resume/CV upload
- **Benefits:** Employee events (local and international), international project opportunities, professional development and training, freedom to choose work tools and devices, hybrid work arrangement, competitive compensation, individual training and certifications
