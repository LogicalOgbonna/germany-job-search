## The Role (Job Description)
You will join the **Growth Insights** team, which is responsible for building a scalable, well-integrated, and governed analytics ecosystem. The team enables Kraken's Growth and Platform teams to make data-driven decisions with confidence. Day-to-day responsibilities include:

- Developing shared libraries ensuring consistent event tracking across multiple codebases
- Building validation and monitoring systems to detect data quality issues proactively
- Creating documentation and establishing best practices for analytics implementation
- Collaborating with Data Engineering and Analytics teams on event data alignment
- Optimizing MarTech infrastructure and integration
- Improving event attribution modeling for user journey insights
- Supporting A/B testing frameworks and experimentation infrastructure

## The Ideal Candidate

**Required Technical Skills:**
- 6+ years frontend development experience
- Deep expertise in React.js, React Native, and TypeScript
- Deep understanding of web and mobile tracking technologies and analytics platforms (Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, Segment, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer)
- Proven ability to audit and troubleshoot complex tracking systems across multiple codebases
- Strong cross-team collaboration experience

**Nice to Have:**
- Frontend testing frameworks and modern build pipelines
- Large-scale data tracking infrastructure experience
- Marketing automation and advertising network knowledge
- A/B testing and experimentation methodologies
- Backend engineering experience
- Cryptocurrency/blockchain understanding

**Soft Skills:**
- Strong communication aligning engineering, marketing, and analytics teams
