# Senior Frontend Engineer - ReactJS (m/f/d)

## Job Title
Senior Frontend Engineer - ReactJS (m/f/d)

## Company Intelligence
**JOIN** is a Berlin-based HR tech company that operates one of Germany's leading multi-posting job platforms. JOIN enables companies to post job listings across multiple job boards simultaneously and manage their recruitment process. The company is well-established in the German market with a growing international presence. As a tech-driven company building their own product, the engineering team works with a modern stack (React, TypeScript, GraphQL, gRPC, Kubernetes) and values end-to-end feature ownership. The team culture emphasizes collaboration between Product, Design, and Engineering.

## The Role (Job Description)
You will take strong ownership of delivering high-impact features end-to-end. Day-to-day responsibilities include:

- Delivering features from technical discovery through production monitoring
- Building complex, maintainable React components and application flows using TypeScript
- Writing robust unit and end-to-end tests for reliability
- Contributing to architectural improvements in state management and performance
- Collaborating with Product and Design teams during discovery phases
- Mentoring less experienced engineers through knowledge sharing and code reviews
- Supporting Design System enhancements when needed

## The Ideal Candidate

**Required Technical Skills:**
- 5+ years frontend development experience with frameworks/UI libraries
- 2+ years React.js and Redux experience
- 2+ years TypeScript proficiency
- 2+ years GraphQL experience

**Nice to Have:**
- JS/TS linters, webpack, or babel expertise
- Cypress or Selenium E2E testing experience
- Storybook or Design System component building
- Monorepo development experience

**Tech Stack:** React.js, TypeScript, Redux, GraphQL, gRPC, Google Pub/Sub, Kubernetes
