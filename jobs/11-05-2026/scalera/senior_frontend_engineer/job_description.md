## The Role (Job Description)

You'll lead frontend development with React and Next.js, building interfaces that make AI-driven procurement workflows accessible and intuitive. You'll use AI tooling to ship at a pace that raises the bar for the team while bringing real UI/UX craft to a complex B2B product. You own the experience end-to-end and define what great looks like at Scalera.

**Day-to-day responsibilities:**
- Lead frontend development with React and Next.js for AI procurement workflows
- Collaborate with backend engineers on API contracts and integrations
- Own frontend performance, accessibility, and code quality, set standards as the product scales
- Work directly with users and customers to translate pain points into product improvements
- Ship features end-to-end: discovery, implementation, testing, production
- Contribute strongly to UI/UX decisions with sharp instincts for complex B2B interfaces

## The Ideal Candidate

- 4+ years of frontend engineering experience with modern JavaScript (vanilla) or frameworks such as React, Vue, SvelteKit, Next.js
- Strong command of modern TypeScript in a complex B2B or technical product context
- Comfortable using AI tooling to accelerate development
- Strong UI/UX instincts and pride in user-facing craft
- Comfortable exploring, iterating, and discovering solutions through experimentation
- Direct user collaboration skills
