# Why are you interested working at HTTPie?

I've used HTTPie CLI for years, and it's the rare dev tool where the defaults are what I actually want. Pretty output, sensible JSON handling, syntax that reads like a sentence. Most HTTP clients optimize for power, HTTPie optimized for the moment a developer stops fighting their tools. That bias toward delight over feature-checklist is what made me look twice at this role.

The work also lines up with what I most enjoy. At Genentech I build interfaces for clinical researchers, technical users who notice when the small things are wrong. Outside of that, I've spent the last year on OurNigeria, a platform I built solo where I designed the API, the SSE streaming chat, and a UI that has to feel trustworthy to citizens querying government budgets in plain English. Drag-and-drop, keyboard shortcuts, real-time collaboration, shared React across web and Electron, those are the exact problems I want to be sitting with at this stage of my career.

Mostly: I want to work on a product where craft is the brief, not something I have to argue for.
