## Company Intelligence

**Mission:** Empower artists and fans to connect and share through music; provide artists with the most progressive tools, services, and resources to build their careers.

**Funding/Stage:** Mature private company; backed by EQT (acquired majority stake in 2023). Operates across New York, Los Angeles, Berlin, and London with a multinational engineering footprint.

**Engineering culture:** Mix of music-product engineering and B2B data products serving the music industry. Strong commitment to AI-native development — the org is explicitly building "agentic" workflows and uses tools like Cursor and Claude Code to multiply engineering velocity. Mature infra-as-code practice (Terraform), modern data stack (BigQuery, Airflow), and emphasis on flat technical leadership.

## The Role (Job Description)
Lead the technical direction for SoundCloud's B2B Insights team, owning the tooling the music industry uses to discover its next stars. You will lead a high-performing, multi-disciplinary squad (Frontend + Data Engineering) focused on the Artist & Repertoire (A&R) Dashboard and Fan Insights — strategic B2B products serving labels, managers, and the music industry.

This is an **IC-heavy leadership role**: drive architecture and execution while remaining hands-on in the code. Reports directly to the Director of Engineering. Designed for someone with the potential and desire to transition into an Engineering Manager role as the team scales.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Own the end-to-end technical roadmap for the A&R and Fan Insights platforms; design scalable, secure systems handling massive datasets at high performance
- Act as technical lead for a small, expert squad — lead by example through architectural clarity, rigorous peer reviews, and high execution momentum
- Partner as a peer to PMs and Designers, translating vague business "wants" into concrete technical "hows"
- Ensure high technical standards across Frontend and Data disciplines; own overall codebase health, consistency, and long-term sustainability
- Champion AI-native development — leverage agentic tools (Cursor, Claude Code) and identify high-value opportunities to deploy agentic capabilities to end users
- Own deployment patterns, infrastructure-as-code (Terraform), and modern security standards
- Leverage agentic workflows and AI-assisted engineering as a force multiplier to ship at "10x the speed of traditional methods"

## The Ideal Candidate

- **Backend:** Proficiency in Scala or Golang; strong understanding of BigData storage and querying (BigQuery, SQL)
