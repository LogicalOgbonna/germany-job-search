# Senior Frontend Developer - React (Remote, CET +/- 3)

## Job Title
Senior Frontend Developer - React (Remote, CET +/- 3) (m/f/d)

## Company Intelligence
**CENDAS GmbH** is a construction-tech SaaS company founded in 2021, transforming the construction industry through digital innovation. Their platform unifies every stakeholder and project phase into a single, streamlined workflow, empowering construction companies to work faster and smarter. Headquartered in Bochum, Germany with a globally distributed team of 60+ professionals. The company embraces an AI-positive culture and remote-first philosophy. They are at the growth stage, building out their engineering team to scale their platform.

## Work Model & Location
**Fully Remote** - Work from anywhere within CET +/- 3 timezone. Optional office available in Bochum, Germany. Flexible working hours.

## The Role (Job Description)
As a Senior Frontend Developer, you will:
- Build and own user-facing features that simplify complex construction workflows
- Design scalable state management solutions for complex application states
- Implement robust offline-first functionality for field work environments (construction sites with limited connectivity)
- Lead code review culture emphasizing knowledge sharing and constructive feedback
- Collaborate with product and design teams to deliver exceptional user experiences
- Drive frontend architecture decisions

## The Ideal Candidate
**Required:**
- 5+ years of hands-on experience building robust, scalable web applications with React & TypeScript
- Deep experience with state management libraries (Zustand, Redux, MobX, Recoil)
- Demonstrated experience with offline-capable PWAs or desktop applications (WatermelonDB, RxDB, AWS AppSync, PouchDB, PowerSync)
- Fluent written and spoken English for async and real-time collaboration
- Strong communication and collaborative problem-solving skills

**Nice-to-Have:**
- Storybook, Design Tokens
- Figma collaboration experience
- Node.js, NestJS, GraphQL
- React Native or Electron experience

**Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, Zustand/Redux, Offline-first (WatermelonDB/RxDB), Storybook, Node.js, NestJS, GraphQL
