## Application Strategy & Intel

### Why This Role Is Interesting

- **Greenfield rebuild:** You are not maintaining legacy code -- you are rebuilding from scratch, which is rare and highly attractive for engineers who want architectural ownership.
- **CTO track:** Explicit mention of a path to Lead Developer or CTO for the right candidate. This is a genuine opportunity at a small, early-stage company.
- **Equity:** Employee equity pool access at an early stage, which could be meaningful if the company scales.
- **Self-funded:** No VC pressure means product decisions are driven by business needs, not investor timelines.
- **AI-driven product:** The company's core product automates media buying with AI, which is a strong market trend.

### Key Angles for Application

1. **Emphasise full-stack JavaScript expertise** -- React/Redux on the frontend, Node.js on the backend. This is the core of what they need.
2. **Highlight any SaaS or platform-building experience** -- they are building tooling, not a consultancy project.
3. **Show architectural thinking** -- they want someone who can make design decisions, not just execute tickets.
4. **Mention any ad-tech, marketing-tech, or e-commerce experience** -- directly relevant to their domain.
5. **Express startup enthusiasm** -- they explicitly value a startup-affine mindset. Show comfort with ambiguity and wearing multiple hats.
6. **AWS/infrastructure experience** -- they need someone comfortable with scalable cloud deployments.

### Red Flags to Watch For

- No salary range disclosed -- clarify compensation expectations early in the process.
- "Early-stage" and "self-funded" -- confirm runway and financial stability during interviews.
- Small team size -- ensure there is adequate support and that expectations around scope are realistic.
- The CTO/Lead Developer offer to a new hire could indicate they currently lack senior technical leadership.
