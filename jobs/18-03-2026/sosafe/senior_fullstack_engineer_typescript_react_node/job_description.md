# Senior Fullstack Engineer - TypeScript/React/Node (m/f/d)

## Job Title
Senior Fullstack Engineer - TypeScript/React/Node (m/f/d)

## Company Intelligence
**SoSafe GmbH** is a leading cybersecurity awareness training platform founded in 2018 in Cologne, Germany. SoSafe helps organizations build a security-aware culture through AI-powered phishing simulations and security awareness training. The company has grown rapidly and is backed by significant venture funding. SoSafe has offices in Cologne (HQ), Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, London, and Lisbon. The company serves enterprise clients across Europe and has a strong engineering culture with a focus on modern web technologies. SoSafe is at a growth/scale-up stage.

## The Role (Job Description)
As a Senior Fullstack Engineer, you will:
- Develop, monitor, and maintain scalable and reliable fullstack software systems using TypeScript (React, Node) in an AWS environment
- Collaborate on the architecture of complex web applications
- Work closely with the platform and frontend teams to ensure seamless integrations
- Design and implement RESTful APIs and microservices
- Contribute to system design and architectural decisions
- Participate in code reviews and knowledge sharing

## The Ideal Candidate
**Required:**
- Minimum 5 years of development experience using modern tech stacks
- At least 3 years of hands-on experience with React & Node.js + TypeScript
- Experience with relational databases, ideally Aurora PostgreSQL
- Solid understanding of RESTful API design and microservices architecture
- Experience with AWS cloud services
- Strong problem-solving skills and collaborative mindset

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React, Node.js, AWS, Aurora PostgreSQL, RESTful APIs, Microservices
