# Founding Engineer (Full-Stack)

## Job Title
Founding Engineer (Full-Stack) / Senior Full-Stack Engineer

## Company Intelligence
**Skalar** is a tax, accounting and payroll AI startup based in Munich, Germany. The company is turning the traditionally painful processes of taxation, accounting and payroll into a radically simple, AI-enabled experience for businesses so they can focus on growth.

- **Founded by experienced entrepreneurs** who have scaled businesses to tens of millions of users, tens of millions in revenue, across multiple countries, and achieved **9-digit exits**.
- **Backed by leading venture investors** and expanding rapidly.
- Technology is at the core of the business -- despite providing professional tax services, the product is fundamentally a tech platform.
- The company is still very early stage (past initial founding, but nearly everything is greenfield).
- Previously branded/associated with the domain skalar.ai (now redirects to makespan.com).

## The Role (Job Description)

### TL;DR
Senior Full-Stack Engineer. Build v1 to v10. Join the early core team of experienced entrepreneurs (scaled to millions of users, $100M+ exits). Build the future of tax & accounting with AI. Ship features end-to-end, influence the stack, and lay the foundations for scale. Grow into leadership as we expand across countries. Above-market pay + meaningful equity.

### The Opportunity
Taxation, accounting and payroll are painful for everyone, yet every business must do them continuously -- it is the backbone the economy runs on. Enabled through AI, Skalar is turning this into a radically simple experience for customers so they can focus on growing their business.

### What You'll Own
- **Build 0 to 1 and beyond with the founders:** Ship major features and product lines end-to-end (spec -> ship -> measure).
- **Core Architecture:** Implement core APIs, data models, and UI; make pragmatic architecture choices with high autonomy.
- **Iterate fast:** Ship v1 fast, iterate weekly with real user feedback.
- **Leadership:** Hire and mentor junior engineers (if interested); gain leadership experience as the company grows.

### Tech Context (Greenfield)
- Prefer boring tech where it matters; innovate where it differentiates.
- **No legacy, no yak-shaving.** Tooling budget for whatever makes you fast.
- You'll have a strong influence on tech and product choices.

- Eye for detail with frontend focus, but also curious about backend

- Deep AI integration into daily coding workflow (not just autocompletion)
- Very good written and verbal communication skills in English (minimum C1 level)
- German language is a plus
- Valid work permit to work in Germany

### What You'll Get
