As a Founding Engineer, you will be one of the first engineering hires, working directly with the founders to build the core product from the ground up.

- Design and develop full-stack product features across frontend, backend, and infrastructure

- Partner directly with founders on product strategy and customer feedback integration

- Participate in recruiting and mentoring future engineering team members

- **AI-native workflow** -- explicit mention of Cursor and Claude Code as required tools signals a team that embraces AI-assisted development. Demonstrating fluency with these tools is a differentiator.
- **Product-engineer archetype** -- repeated emphasis on "product-obsessed," customer feedback integration, and concept-to-production speed. They want builders, not ticket-takers.
- **Small team (1-10)** -- you will have outsized influence but also outsized responsibility. Every contribution is visible.

### Application Approach

1. **Lead with startup experience** -- highlight any early-stage or founding engineer experience where you wore multiple hats and shipped fast.
2. **Showcase AI/LLM work** -- any experience with LLM APIs, prompt engineering, vector databases, or conversational AI should be front and center.
3. **Demonstrate product thinking** -- frame past work in terms of user outcomes and business impact, not just technical implementation.
4. **Highlight the full stack** -- show depth across Next.js, Node.js/Python, PostgreSQL, and cloud infrastructure (Azure or transferable cloud experience).
5. **Cover letter optional but recommended** -- given the founding nature of the role, a brief note explaining why you want to build in the AI sales training space would stand out.
6. **Direct contact available** -- Lennard Greve (lennard.greve@sleak.ai) is listed as the contact person. Candidates may ask questions before applying.
