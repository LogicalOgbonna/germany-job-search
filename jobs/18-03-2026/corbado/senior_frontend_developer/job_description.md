# Senior Frontend Developer

## Company Intelligence

**Corbado** is an early-stage startup based in Munich on a mission to free the world from passwords through innovative passkey authentication solutions. They build developer-facing SDKs and tools that enable passwordless authentication across web applications.

- **Product**: Passkey-based authentication platform with JavaScript SDKs supporting multiple frontend frameworks (React, Next.js, Vue.js, Nuxt, Svelte, Angular). They focus on developer experience and framework-agnostic solutions via Vanilla JavaScript.
- **Funding**: Angel-backed, supported by experienced advisors. No disclosed institutional round yet.
- **Team**: Founded by serial entrepreneurs with successful exits and proven scaling experience at major German internet companies. Team includes experienced CTOs and founders.
- **Traction**: A couple of hundred developer signups monthly, indicating solid developer community adoption.
- **Media Recognition**: Featured by TechCrunch, The Verge, and heise.

- Lead frontend development of passkey solutions while collaborating with backend teams

- Adapt solutions to emerging frameworks to expand developer ecosystem reach
- Drive integration into next-generation platforms (Vercel, Cloudflare Workers) leveraging SSR and Next.js 13 component-based architecture
