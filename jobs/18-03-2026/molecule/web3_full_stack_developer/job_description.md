# Web3 Full Stack Developer

## Job Title
Web3 Full Stack Developer

## Company Intelligence
**Molecule** (operating as BIO.xyz) is at the heart of the Decentralized Science (DeSci) movement, where biotech meets blockchain to democratize scientific research. The company has raised $20+ million in funding and is backed by notable figures in the web3 world, including Vitalik Buterin, Balaji Srinivasan, and Juan Benet. Notable projects include VitaDAO (the largest biotech DAO with $4M+ deployed for longevity research) and the IP-NFT protocol for tokenizing scientific intellectual property. Molecule is a pioneer in the DeSci space, headquartered in Berlin with a globally distributed team.

## Work Model & Location
**Remote** - Berlin, Germany headquarters with remote work option. Globally distributed team.

## The Role (Job Description)
As a Web3 Full Stack Developer, you will:
- Design and build scalable front-end and back-end systems for the Bio Protocol platform
- Create intuitive UIs for researchers and agents to submit tasks, manage funding, and view experiment outcomes
- Develop and integrate Ethereum smart contracts for $BIO token transactions and staking mechanisms
- Build RESTful and GraphQL APIs connecting front-end interfaces with agent systems
- Implement decentralized (IPFS) and traditional databases for metadata and reputation tracking
- Ensure comprehensive security standards across all platform components
- Work at the intersection of AI, blockchain, and biotech

## The Ideal Candidate
**Required:**
- 3+ years of fullstack development experience
- Strong proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript, Node.js, React.js (or Vue.js)
- Blockchain experience: Solidity, Hardhat
- Smart contract development (ERC-20, staking mechanisms)
- Experience with Ethereum Layer 2 solutions (Polygon, Optimism, Arbitrum)
- Database experience: MongoDB, PostgreSQL, IPFS

**Nice-to-Have:**
- AI/ML integration with web applications
- Open-source blockchain or scientific contributions

**Tech Stack:** React.js, Node.js, TypeScript, Solidity, Hardhat, GraphQL, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, IPFS, Ethereum/L2s
