## The Role (Job Description)

### Team & Mission

The Insights team is part of **AI Agents Advanced** — one of Zendesk's most critical product pillars. Zendesk is on a mission to build the most advanced AI agents in CX, and the Insights team is focused on delivering intelligent analytics, contextual insights, and decision-support tools that empower users to take meaningful action.

### What You'll Do

As a Fullstack Engineer on this team, you will be instrumental in building features that harness artificial intelligence to help users better understand, interpret, and act on complex information.

While this is a fullstack role, the Germany-based version **leans more on frontend development** — they are looking for someone who is passionate about crafting user-centric interfaces and rich visualizations, while also being comfortable contributing to backend services, and owning delivery end-to-end.

You will work closely with product managers, designers, data engineers, data analysts, and fellow developers to create seamless, intuitive experiences — combining rich data, intelligent systems, and responsive UIs.

### Key Responsibilities

- Develop features leveraging AI to help users understand and act on complex information
- Build user-centric interfaces with rich data visualizations
- Contribute to backend services alongside frontend work
- Own end-to-end delivery across the full stack
- Collaborate cross-functionally with product managers, designers, data engineers, data analysts, and developers
- Shape how customers make informed decisions using real-time AI-powered insights
- Contribute to system reliability through code quality and testing

### Tech Stack

### What Success Looks Like

- Contribute to system reliability and maintainability through code quality and testing
- Collaborate effectively across disciplines and participate actively in team processes
- Demonstrate ownership and proactively seek feedback
- Show technical growth and increasing independence over time
