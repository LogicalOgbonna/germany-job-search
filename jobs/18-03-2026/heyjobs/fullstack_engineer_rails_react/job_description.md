# Fullstack Engineer (Rails & React) - B2B Recruiter Portal (f/m/d)

## Job Title
Fullstack Engineer (Rails & React) - B2B Recruiter Portal (f/m/d)

## Company Intelligence
**HeyJobs** is a Berlin-based HR-tech company that uses machine-learning technologies to connect people to their next job and enable recruiters to fill open positions with perfectly matching candidates. The company has raised over €50M in funding, serves more than 1,700 enterprise customers, and has a team of 400+ across offices in Berlin, Cologne, Hamburg, and Vienna. Their vision is to become the trustworthy career advisor for talent in Europe. The company is at a scale-up stage with strong revenue and a clear path to profitability.

## Work Model & Location
**Hybrid** - Berlin, Germany (office in Berlin-Kreuzberg). Flexible working hours with hybrid arrangements.

## The Role (Job Description)
As a Fullstack Engineer on the Recruiter Portal Team, you will:
- Design, implement, and maintain significant parts of user flows and backend mechanisms for the B2B recruiter platform
- Build and run the platform that helps recruiters create and manage job openings to attract the perfect candidates
- Work across the full stack with Rails backend and React frontend
- Contribute to product and engineering decisions in a collaborative team environment
- Help HeyJobs achieve its mission of connecting 200 million non-academic talent across Europe with career opportunities

## The Ideal Candidate
**Required:**
- Strong experience with Ruby on Rails
- Proficiency with React for frontend development
- Full-stack development mindset
- Experience building and maintaining user-facing web applications
- Agile development experience

**Tech Stack:** Ruby on Rails, React, JavaScript/TypeScript
