# Frontend Software Engineer

## Company Intelligence

**Company:** Emidat GmbH
**Founded:** 2023, Munich, Germany
**Founders:** Lisa Oberaigner (CEO) & Florian Fesch (CTO)
**Funding:** EUR 4 million seed round led by General Catalyst
**Industry:** Climate Tech / Construction / B2B SaaS

**What Emidat Does:**
Emidat is building the first AI-powered, climate-native product management system for building material manufacturers. Their core product automates the generation, verification, and publication of Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) -- reducing the process from ~12 months and ~EUR 15,000 to approximately one week and a few hundred euros per product. Their database covers environmental data on over 150,000 construction products, accessible via API and UI.

**Mission:** Decarbonize building materials, which account for 11% of global greenhouse gas emissions. They provide transparency on the environmental impact of construction materials and help manufacturers comply with EU regulatory deadlines.

**Clients:** Three of the world's largest concrete manufacturers, plus leading building material companies across concrete, insulation, steel, and facade industries in Europe.

**Mentorship Network:** Former CTOs from PayWorks, StashAway, and Eventbrite.

## The Role

This is Emidat's **first full-time frontend engineering hire** -- a high-impact, high-ownership position. You will own and scale the entire frontend of the Emidat platform, working directly with Florian (Co-Founder & CTO) and a team of five software engineers plus one existing frontend engineer.

You are expected to act as a **Product Engineer**, bringing the frontend perspective to feature development and platform architecture, collaborating closely with the full-stack team.

### Responsibilities (First 6 Months)

- Assume full ownership of production frontend code, transitioning from the current contractor
- Learn the codebase, customer requirements, and LCA (Life Cycle Assessment) compliance needs
- Discover, conceptualize, build, and ship features to maintain the highest-level user experience on the Emidat platform
- Leverage AI tooling (Claude Code, Cursor) to enhance development workflows
- Develop scalable frontend architecture
- Collaborate on API design and backend integration
