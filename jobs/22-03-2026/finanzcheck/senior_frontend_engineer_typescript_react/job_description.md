## The Role (Job Description)
You will develop React frontend solutions within a data-intensive, distributed system. The role involves building features that integrate with NodeJS Serverless and Golang backend services running on AWS Lambda and Kubernetes. You'll participate in team decision-making on technical direction and tooling, collaborating with Product Owners and UX Designers.

### Key Responsibilities
- Develop high-performance React frontend solutions in a data-intensive, distributed system

- Participate in team decision-making on technical direction and tooling
- Collaborate with Product Owners and UX Designers on feature development
- Own products end-to-end under "you build it, you run it, you own it" philosophy
