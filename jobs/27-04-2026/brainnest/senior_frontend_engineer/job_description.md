## The Role (Job Description)
The Senior Frontend Engineer at Brainnest owns delivery of customer-facing product features across web and mobile (React + React Native) while raising the engineering bar for the broader frontend team. Day to day you will:
- Deliver functionality reliably and on a regular cadence to production.
- Author automated tests (unit, integration, end-to-end) so that releases stay safe at scale.
- Contribute to architectural decisions: state management approach, module boundaries, build/release pipeline, and component-library evolution.
- Guide the team on best practices, framework evaluation, and code-review standards.
- Partner with engineering management on scalable, maintainable approaches to new product surfaces.
- Pair across web and mobile — touching React Native (animations, gestures, deep linking) as needed, and stepping into native Android/iOS compilation when required.

## The Ideal Candidate
**Required**
- 4+ years of professional software development.
- 3+ years specifically in frontend web development.
- 3+ years of JavaScript and ReactJS.
- 2+ years of TypeScript or Flow.
- 2+ years of Redux for state management.
- 1+ years of React Native (animations, gestures, deep linking).
- Working knowledge of Android and iOS compilation/packaging fundamentals.
- Proven habit of writing automated tests as part of normal feature work.

**Soft Skills**
- Comfortable mentoring mid/junior engineers.
- Opinionated about code quality but pragmatic about shipping.
- Strong written/verbal English (team is globally distributed).
