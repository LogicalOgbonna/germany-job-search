# Senior Full-Stack Engineer — Open Energy Transition (OET)

## Job Title
Senior Full-Stack Software Engineer (Global | Remote | English)

## Company Intelligence
**Open Energy Transition (OET)** is a remote-first **non-profit** building open-source tools and data for energy and grid planning. Mission: make energy planning more transparent, reproducible, and accessible — and push open approaches to become the global standard by 2030. The product surface includes web apps, dashboards, data tools, and internal platforms that wrap real modelling workflows used by energy and policy researchers. OET ships in the open: documentation, contributor workflows, and upstreaming improvements to upstream OSS projects are explicit expectations of the role. Funding model is grant + partnership + engagement rather than VC, so engineering decisions are weighted toward durability, reproducibility, and open collaboration over feature velocity. This is not a typical SaaS gig — it is mission-driven climate-tech, and the bar emphasizes open-source contribution history and quality writing.

## The Role (Job Description)
A hands-on senior IC role that spans frontend, backend, and data layer. Expect:
- Build and evolve **web applications and dashboards** for results exploration, reporting, and operational tools.
- Improve and maintain **existing platforms** — refactors, performance, UX work, and reliability investments.
- Design and implement **APIs and data models** so that data is easy to query, audit, and reuse.
- Create **automation**: scripts, tooling, and reproducible environments that reduce manual work.
- Integrate with **Python-first pipelines** and modelling workflows (ETLs, orchestration with Snakemake, Apache Airflow, or similar).
- Own quality via **CI/CD**, pragmatic testing, and stable deployments.
- Communicate clearly in a remote-first team — async docs, high-signal PRs, thoughtful reviews.
- **Open-source citizenship:** ship and maintain OSS responsibly with strong documentation, tests, contributor workflows, and a default to upstreaming improvements when it makes sense.

## The Ideal Candidate

- Bachelor's in CS, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience.
