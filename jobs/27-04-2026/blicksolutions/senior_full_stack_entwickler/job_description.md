# Senior Full Stack Entwickler/in (100% Remote) — BlickSolutions

## Job Title
Senior Full Stack Entwickler/in (100% Remote)

## Company Intelligence
**BlickSolutions GmbH** is a Germany-based software house specializing in **Shopify ecosystem development** — apps, plugins, and storefront integrations — that serve **3,500+ merchants** in production. The team operates as a small, execution-focused engineering shop on the Shopify App Store, where reliability and merchant trust translate directly to recurring revenue. Engineering culture is explicitly framed around **execution, speed, and accountability** — i.e., a flat, high-ownership team where senior engineers ship with minimal management overhead. Funding is bootstrapped/cash-flow driven (Shopify partner economy), so decisions skew pragmatic over architecturally maximalist. The role is 100% remote within Germany — no Berlin commute, but you must be eligible to work in Germany.

## The Role (Job Description)
Hands-on senior IC role spanning the full Shopify-app stack:
- **Architecture and development** of Shopify apps, plugins, and API integrations end-to-end.
- **Frontend:** Build with React and Next.js. Implement responsive, accessible UI components.
- **Backend:** Node.js with Express. Integrate with third-party systems via REST and GraphQL.
- **Performance:** Optimize for speed, scale, and merchant-facing latency.
- **Quality:** Code reviews, unit/integration/E2E testing, debugging.
- **Operations:** Build CI/CD pipelines and maintain technical documentation.
- **Collaboration:** Work cross-functionally with design, product, and QA.
- **Security:** Implement OAuth and Shopify auth flows correctly.
