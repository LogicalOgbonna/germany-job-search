# Senior Frontend Engineer — Remote (remote.com)

## Job Title
Senior Frontend Engineer

## Company Intelligence
**Remote** is the global category-defining HR / Employer-of-Record platform that lets companies hire, pay, and manage international employees compliantly across virtually every country. The company is fully distributed (Remoters work from 6 continents — "Antarctica left to go"), highly async, and openly publishes its compensation philosophy and engineering practices. Engineering is organized into cross-functional verticals across Frontend, Backend (Elixir/Phoenix), SRE, and QA, deploying multiple times per day on AWS via GitLab CI/CD. Remote has aggressively wired AI / agentic workflows into its engineering operating model: every role now includes a stated expectation around using and building agentic workflows. Reputation is that of a well-funded, mature scale-up with strong async-writing culture and an engineering bar that explicitly evaluates "innovation." The role is funded across multiple teams — final team assignment happens during interviews based on business need and your preferences.

## The Role (Job Description)
You will join one of Remote's cross-functional product verticals — exact team to be matched during interviews. Across teams the role is consistent:
- **Lead delivery of major team-scoped projects** and contribute to cross-team initiatives across Remote's HR and Payroll products.
- Participate in product work — give feedback, propose solutions, and use technical insight to suggest product improvements rather than just implementing tickets.
- Maintain deep understanding of the team's product domain and business context.
- Provide rigorous code reviews and contribute to the shared codebase.
- Debug and resolve technical and business issues end-to-end.
- Participate in non-team activities: support rotations, hiring loops, RFC discussions.
- Mentor and provide guidance to other engineers.
- Investigate, propose, and implement platform-level improvements.
- Build interfaces optimized for performance, accessibility, and clean API design.
- **Agentic-workflow scope (stated requirement):** Redesign how engineering work ships using autonomous agents as the default execution layer. Operationalize agentic workflows end-to-end (spec → plan → execute → verify), build reusable agentic primitives in the codebase, and use verification loops (tests, checks, evals, guardrails) to keep agent output correct, secure, and reliable.

## The Ideal Candidate
**Required**
- Previous experience as a Frontend Engineer / Developer.
- Solid JavaScript ES6.
- Proficiency with React.
- Jest and React Testing Library or Enzyme — writing meaningful tests, not just snapshot noise.
- Track record leading implementation and maintenance of large frontend applications.
- Comfort taking abstract concepts and turning them into shipping software.
- Attention to detail for accessible, polished experiences.
- Strong independence and self-direction (async culture).
- Curiosity and active willingness to develop.
- Fluency in English.
