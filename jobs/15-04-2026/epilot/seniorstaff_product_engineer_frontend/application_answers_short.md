# Short Application Answers

## Why do you want to start at epilot?
epilot stands out to me because it combines a meaningful mission with the kind of product engineering environment I enjoy most. I like building software that simplifies complex workflows, and helping make energy products easier to sell and manage digitally is a problem with real impact. I am also drawn to epilot's focus on ownership, API-first thinking, and close collaboration between engineering, product, and design.

## What achievement in your career are you particularly proud of? Why?
I am particularly proud of leading the redesign of a core clinical-trial product at Genentech, migrating it from a Vue.js web application to a React Chrome extension while still supporting the legacy app during the transition. I am proud of it because it required balancing product delivery, technical migration, performance improvements, and release quality at the same time.

## Which of our engineering principles appeals to you the most and why?
The principle that appeals to me most is ownership, especially the idea of "you build it, you run it." I like being responsible not just for implementing features, but also for performance, testing, reliability, and the overall quality of what reaches users. That mindset matches how I have worked in my recent roles.
