# Frontend Engineer - HyperDX

## Company Intelligence

**ClickHouse** is the company behind the world's fastest open-source column-oriented database, purpose-built for real-time analytics. Named to the **2025 Forbes Cloud 100** list, ClickHouse has established itself as a dominant force in the data infrastructure space.

- **Funding:** Recently closed a **$400M Series D** round, signaling massive investor confidence and growth trajectory.
- **Scale:** 3,000+ customers across 20 countries with **250% year-over-year ARR growth**.
- **Notable Customers:** Capital One, Meta, Cursor, Sony, Tesla.
- **Engineering Culture:** Strong open-source DNA. The company actively engages with a large developer community and open-source contributors. HyperDX itself is an open-source observability platform they acquired/built on top of ClickHouse.
- **Product (HyperDX):** A petabyte-scale observability platform that unifies logs, metrics, traces, and session replays into a single interface, helping engineers debug production issues faster. Think Datadog competitor built natively on ClickHouse.

## The Role (Job Description)

You will be building and owning key features across the HyperDX observability platform, a petabyte-scale system that integrates logs, metrics, traces, and session replays. This is a high-impact, full-stack leaning frontend role where you will:

- **Shape the product:** Own and build key features, focusing on frontend and backend systems for the observability platform.
- **Engage with the community:** Collaborate directly with open-source contributors and enterprise customers to understand needs and iterate rapidly.
- **Performance at scale:** Optimize the platform to handle massive data volumes efficiently, ensuring smooth UX even at petabyte scale.
- **Debug production issues:** Build the tools that help other engineers debug their production systems faster.
