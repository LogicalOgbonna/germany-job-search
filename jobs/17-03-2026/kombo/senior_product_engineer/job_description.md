# Senior Product Engineer

## Job Title
Senior Product Engineer

## Company Intelligence
**Kombo** is a Y Combinator S22 company building a unified API for HR, payroll, and recruiting systems. Kombo enables engineering teams to integrate with 100+ HR/payroll/recruiting platforms through a single API, eliminating the need for dozens of separate integrations. The company has achieved **$8M+ ARR within 24 months** post-YC, employs **45+ people**, and has raised **$8M+ in funding** from Y Combinator and 468 Capital. Kombo processes data for approximately **10% of Germany's workforce** — a remarkable scale indicator for a company of this size. They ship **20+ releases per day**, signaling a high-velocity engineering culture.
