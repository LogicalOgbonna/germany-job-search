## Job Title
Senior Fullstack Engineer - Support and AI (F/M/D)

## Company Intelligence
**Freenow** (formerly mytaxi) is Europe's leading mobility super-app, offering ride-hailing, car sharing, scooters, and other mobility services across multiple European cities. The company is a joint venture backed by **BMW and Mercedes-Benz**, giving it substantial financial backing and automotive industry connections. Freenow processes millions of trips and serves as a critical mobility platform in major European markets. The "Support and AI" team suggests work on AI-powered customer support and operational tools.
