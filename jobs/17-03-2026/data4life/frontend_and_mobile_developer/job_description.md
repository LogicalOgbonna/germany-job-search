# Frontend and Mobile Developer (f/m/x)

## Job Title
Frontend and Mobile Developer (f/m/x)

## Company Intelligence
**Data4Life** is a nonprofit digital health organization based in Berlin/Potsdam. The company builds digital health solutions focused on accessibility, performance, and security. Data4Life works on innovative health technology including wearable sensor connectivity, mobile health applications, and Wear OS companion apps. As a nonprofit, the mission is impact-driven rather than profit-driven, offering meaningful work in the health-tech space.

- **Location:** Berlin, Germany (with occasional presence required in Potsdam office)

## The Role (Job Description)
- Develop frontend and mobile features using accessible, performant, maintainable, and well-tested code
- Implement Bluetooth Low Energy modules for wearable sensor connectivity
- Build sensor integration modules for mobile phones, smartwatches, and wearables via SDKs
- Extend existing Wear OS companion app (Kotlin)
- Contribute to UI/UX design for digital health products
- Collaborate with UX Design, Backend Engineering, QA, and Product Management teams

## The Ideal Candidate
### Required Skills
- 2+ years frontend engineering experience
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- Android and optional iOS mobile app development experience
- **React Native** hands-on experience
- Strong proficiency in HTML, CSS, **JavaScript/TypeScript**
- Focus on performance, semantic markup, accessibility, and security
- Fluent English; German knowledge preferred

### Tech Stack
- React Native (cross-platform development)
- TypeScript/JavaScript
- HTML & CSS
- Kotlin (Wear OS development)
- Bluetooth Low Energy protocols
