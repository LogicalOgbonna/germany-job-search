# Mid-Senior Full-Stack Web Engineer (m/w/d)

## Job Title
Mid-Senior Full-Stack Web Engineer (m/w/d)

## Company Intelligence
**Exolaunch** is a global leader in smallsat launch and deployment services, headquartered at Potsdamer Platz in Berlin. The company provides rideshare launch, in-orbit transportation, deployment, and integration services for the NewSpace industry. Exolaunch has deployed hundreds of satellites and works with major launch providers. This is a unique opportunity to work in the space industry with a modern web stack, building mission-critical software for satellite operations.

- **Location:** Must reside in Berlin or willing to relocate with valid German work authorization
