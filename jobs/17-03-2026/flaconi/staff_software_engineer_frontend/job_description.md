## Job Title
Staff Software Engineer – Front-End (all genders)

**flaconi** is one of Germany's leading online beauty and cosmetics retailers, operating a high-traffic e-commerce storefront. The company runs a modern Next.js App Router-based storefront and is investing heavily in frontend architecture modernization. flaconi operates in the competitive beauty e-commerce space with a strong brand presence in the DACH region. The Staff-level title indicates a high-impact, architecture-shaping role with cross-team influence.
