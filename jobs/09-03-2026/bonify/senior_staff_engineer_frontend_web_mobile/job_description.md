## The Role (Job Description)
This role goes beyond feature delivery — you will modernize frontend architecture, raise engineering standards, and help shape how frontend systems scale over time. You will act as a force multiplier across teams.

**Core Responsibilities:**
- Lead modernization of frontend architecture, emphasizing mobile app design, performance, and scalability
- Evaluate and evolve frontend system design (application structure, state management, data flow)
- Drive improvements in performance, reliability, accessibility, and user experience
- Establish best practices for development, testing, and build systems
- Reduce maintenance costs through simplification and standardization
- Partner with backend, product, and design teams on cohesive end-to-end experiences
- Enhance developer experience via tooling and architectural guidance
- Contribute high-leverage code for foundational components and refactors
- Mentor senior engineers and influence quality through reviews
