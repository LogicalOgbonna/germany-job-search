velio is a bicycle/mobility startup operating in the e-commerce and marketplace space. The company is building a B2C platform for cycling enthusiasts, focused on creating brilliant customer journeys. velio operates as a remote-first company with a base in Cologne, Germany. The company is at an early/growth stage — they describe themselves as "forming the company from the ground up," offering significant growth potential and direct product impact.
