## Job Title
Senior Frontend Engineer

Your mission
As a Senior Frontend Engineer at mula, you are the driving force behind our user interface and the primary authority for our frontend architecture. Together with our backend specialists, you will take full responsibility for transforming our vision into a high-performance, scalable, and intuitive digital experience. With at least four years of experience, you will help us bridge the gap between complex backend logic and a seamless B2B user journey, ensuring we remain the world's most integrated merchandise platform.
Your role
You will be  responsible for the entire frontend codebase  and define the technical direction of our client-side applications.

You will design and implement  scalable frontend architectures that work perfectly with our Django-based backend.

You will work closely with two backend engineers to   define API contracts and ensure a smooth data flow across the entire stack.

You translate design mockups into  pixel-perfect, responsive and accessible interfaces that delight our B2B customers.

Your tasks
Feature development: You  will lead the development of our core web applications using  React  and  Next.js.

State Management & Data Fetching:  You manage complex application states with  React Query  and  React Context .

Integration:  You will use and design RESTful APIs and ensure efficient data handling and error management.

Performance optimization:  You keep our platform fast and reliable – through code splitting, caching strategies and modern CSS practices.

Quality & Standards:  You maintain a clean, modular codebase and establish frontend best practices (testing, documentation and CI/CD).

Cross-functional collaboration:  You work hand in hand with product and design teams to translate requirements into technical reality.

Your profile
4 years of professional experience  in frontend development with a deep focus on the  React ecosystem .

Expert knowledge of Next.js , including a solid understanding of SSR, SSG, and ISR patterns.

TypeScript expert:  You use TypeScript safely to ensure type safety throughout the entire application.

State Management Expert:  You have in-depth experience handling complex data via  Redux  and a strong preference for  React Query .

Testing experience:  Confident handling of testing frameworks such as Jest, React Testing Library or Cypress.

Modern CSS Mastery:  In-depth knowledge of responsive design and modern styling approaches (e.g.,  Styled Components  or  CSS Modules ).

Agile Mindset:  You feel comfortable in a lean, dynamic environment where personal responsibility is the norm.

Communication:  You can explain technical frontend concepts to non-technical stakeholders in a way that is easy to understand, and you clearly document your architectural decisions.

Languages:  Fluent English (spoken and written).

Residence:  Berlin.
