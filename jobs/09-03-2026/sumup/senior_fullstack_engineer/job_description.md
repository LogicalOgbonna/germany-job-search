# (Senior) Fullstack Engineer

## Job Title
(Senior) Fullstack Engineer

## Company Intelligence
**Company:** SumUp

SumUp is a global fintech company and one of the world's leading payment technology providers, empowering millions of small businesses worldwide. Founded in 2012, SumUp has grown to serve 4+ million merchants globally and has raised over $1.5 billion in funding. The company is headquartered in London with a major engineering hub in Berlin. SumUp offers card readers, POS systems, and a full merchant dashboard. The engineering culture emphasizes ownership, collaboration, and modern technology practices.
