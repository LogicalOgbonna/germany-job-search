# Senior Frontend Engineer

## Job Title
Senior Frontend Engineer

## Company Intelligence
**Company:** Personio

Personio is one of Europe's most valuable HR tech companies, building an all-in-one HR platform for small and mid-sized businesses. The company has raised over EUR 700 million in funding, reaching a valuation of $8.5 billion at its peak, with investors including Greenoaks Capital, Altimeter Capital, and Index Ventures. Personio serves over 12,000 customers across Europe and employs 1,800+ people. The engineering team is large and well-structured, working with micro-frontends, mono-repositories, and modern React/TypeScript patterns on AWS. Pre-IPO equity is offered, signaling potential upside. This is a well-funded scale-up with the stability of a mature company and the equity upside of pre-IPO participation.

## Work Model & Location
- **Hybrid:** 50% remote, 50% in-office
- **Office Locations:** Munich (preferred) or Berlin
- **Type:** Full-time

## The Role (Job Description)
- Build elegant, scalable, and high-performing frontend components using React, Next.js, and TypeScript
- Work in an autonomous, cross-functional feature-based team (PET team — People Experience Technology)
- Take UX and UI to the next level in close collaboration with designers and product owners
- Drive efficiency for HR managers in managing core employee data and key lifecycle events
- Ensure employees can easily access and govern their own data
- Improve the frontend codebase (primarily React) and run it efficiently on AWS infrastructure
- Train and coach peers and support hiring decisions
- Play a role in shaping engineering culture

## The Ideal Candidate
**Experience:** 5+ years of frontend engineering

**Technical Skills (Required):**
- React, TypeScript, and modern frontend architectures
- Client-side and server-side rendering (CSR, SSR)
- Optimizing Critical Render Path (CRP) and CSS
- Micro-frontends and mono-repositories
- Frontend developer experience tools
- Mastery of front-end frameworks (React preferred)

**Nice-to-Have:**
- AWS infrastructure experience
- Next.js expertise
