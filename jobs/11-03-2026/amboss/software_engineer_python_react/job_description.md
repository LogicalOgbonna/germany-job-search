# Software Engineer (Python/React)

## Job Title
Software Engineer (Python/React) — ExamPrep Team

## Company Intelligence
**AMBOSS GmbH** is a globally recognized medical education and clinical decision-support platform used by millions of medical students and clinicians worldwide. Headquartered in **Berlin**, with offices in Cologne, New York, Cagliari (Sardinia), and Cape Town. AMBOSS is a well-funded, mission-driven company at the intersection of health-tech and ed-tech, known for its strong engineering culture, product-minded teams, and excellent workplace perks (including an in-house chef). The engineering org is organized into autonomous product teams with high ownership.

## The Role (Job Description)
You'll join the **ExamPrep Team**, building personalized learning experiences for medical students. Key responsibilities:
- Own features end-to-end from implementation through deployment
- Develop prototypes that evolve into production systems
- Contribute to roadmap strategy alongside product managers, designers, and medical editors
- Become proficient in a complex product domain (medical education)
- Build and maintain APIs (GraphQL & REST) and frontend interfaces

## The Ideal Candidate
**Required:**
- Strong **Python** backend development experience
- **React/JavaScript/TypeScript** frontend skills (growth potential welcome)
- API design and integration experience (GraphQL & REST)
- Database design and data modeling expertise
- Testing and observability practices
- Docker and CI/CD pipeline familiarity
- Product-minded mindset prioritizing user impact
- Data-driven product development philosophy
- Proficiency with AI-assisted tools (Cursor, GitHub Copilot)

**Tech Stack:** Python, FastAPI, React, TypeScript, GraphQL, REST, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS (EKS)
