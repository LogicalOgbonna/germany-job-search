## Company Intelligence
**Certivity** is a Munich-based **RegTech startup** using AI to automate regulatory compliance for product manufacturers. Founded recently, they have grown from 4 founders to **75+ employees** in just three years, serving over **1,500 users across 10 countries**. The company is well-funded (backed by HTGF among others) and operates at the intersection of AI, legal-tech, and SaaS. The engineering team is small (~5 people in the core tech team), meaning high impact and visibility for each engineer. Flat hierarchies and high autonomy are core cultural values.
