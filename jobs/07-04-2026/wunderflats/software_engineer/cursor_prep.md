## Overall Strategy for the Technical Rounds
* **The "Contract Engine" Context:** Keep in mind that you'll be working on complex financial and contractual systems. When discussing technical choices, always tie them back to reliability, data consistency, and handling edge cases.
* **The "Buy/Integrate over Build" Mindset:** In all three interviews, emphasize that your first instinct is to leverage existing tools (like you did at OurNigeria) rather than building custom solutions from scratch, unless absolutely necessary for the core business logic.
* **AI Connection:** Since both Behnam and Agnieszka won an AI hackathon at Wunderflats, your recent experience building an AI-powered platform at OurNigeria is a massive advantage. Make sure to highlight the technical challenges you solved there (e.g., hybrid RAG pipelines, multi-agent systems, streaming responses).
