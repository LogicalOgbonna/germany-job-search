# Software Engineer (f/m/d) — Wunderflats

## Job Title
Software Engineer (f/m/d)

## Company Intelligence
**Wunderflats** helps people find fully furnished homes for a month or more, perfect for relocations, family moves, or life transitions. Founded in Berlin in 2015 by Jan Hase and Arkadi Jampolski, it bridges the gap between verified landlords and carefully vetted tenants. 
- **Scale:** Over 63,000 apartments listed across Europe, with a team of over 150 people.
- **Engineering Culture:** Highly pragmatic, product-obsessed, and leverage-focused. They explicitly value "Buy/Integrate over Build" and want engineers who measure worth by business value rather than lines of code. Lean-Agile thinking is treated as a survival mechanism, and they expect adaptability as priorities shift.
- **Mission:** Building and scaling the engine of trust — their core payments and pricing platform.

## Work Model & Location
- **Location:** Berlin, Germany (Hackescher Markt office)
- **Work Model:** Hybrid. Main working hours are spent in the office, but employees are welcome to work from home from time to time. Flexible and family-friendly hours.

## The Role (Job Description)
You will own mission-critical financial systems from the database to the API to the user interface, with a core focus on the backend.
- **Key Project:** "The Contract Engine" — automating 95% of all contracts across Europe (DE, AT, FR, BE, LU, CH, IT, PT, ES), navigating local regulations.
- **Approach:** Write as little code as possible. Map out third-party providers, build flawless integrations, and solve massive business problems with surgical precision.
- **Ownership:** Take features from an idea on a whiteboard to a deployed, monitored service.

## The Ideal Candidate
**Mindset & Soft Skills:**
- **Systems Thinker:** Models systems, thinks about race conditions, data consistency, and edge cases before they become production incidents.
- **High "Say-Do" Ratio:** Accountable, commits and delivers, communicates blockers with clarity, focuses on outcomes.
- **Product-Obsessed:** Understands the "why", pushes back on requirements that don't make sense, finds the simplest solution.
- **Thrives on Ownership:** Wants to own a problem, not just a ticket.

**Core Technical Strengths:**
- **Backend:** Significant professional experience building and shipping applications using **Node.js** and **TypeScript**. Ability to build robust, scalable backend services.
- **Frontend:** Proficient with modern **React** (with JS/TS) and experience building/maintaining user-facing applications. **Next.js** is a strong plus.

**Nice to Have (Bonus):**
- **Platform:** GCP, Kubernetes, Terraform, GitHub Actions
- **Data:** MongoDB Atlas
- **API:** GraphQL (Apollo/Nexus), some REST
