# Senior Full Stack Developer

**Job Title:** Senior Full Stack Developer

## Company Intelligence

**Company:** MOBIKO GmbH
**Core Product/Mission:** MOBIKO is a B2B SaaS platform for corporate mobility management — it helps companies manage, allocate, and report on employee mobility budgets across transport modes (public transit, bike, car, rideshare). The platform replaces legacy company car models with flexible, sustainable mobility allowances.
**Stage:** Growth-stage startup (~30 people), Munich-based, established product with a paying customer base across Germany.
**Engineering Culture:** Remote-first culture, small team with high ownership. Engineers work across the full product stack and are expected to improve test coverage, maintain code quality, and introduce new technologies as needed.
**Stack Profile:** Modern TypeScript-first shop — NestJS on the backend, Vue/React on the frontend, PostgreSQL, deployed on Azure with Kubernetes.

## The Role (Job Description)

As a Senior Full Stack Developer at MOBIKO, you will:

- Build and maintain product features across the TypeScript stack (Vue.js/React frontend + NestJS backend)
- Expand and improve automated test coverage using Jest
- Maintain and enforce code quality standards with modern tooling
- Collaborate creatively with the broader tech team on architecture and feature decisions
- Contribute to DevOps responsibilities: Docker, Kubernetes on Azure, Terraform, Flux, Helm, ArgoCD
- Take ownership of features from design to production deployment
