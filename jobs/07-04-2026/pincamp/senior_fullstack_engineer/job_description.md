# Senior Fullstack Engineer (m/f/d) — PiNCAMP

## Job Title
Senior Fullstack Engineer (m/f/d) Travel E-Commerce in Berlin

## Company Intelligence
**PiNCAMP** is the camping platform of ADAC (Germany's largest automotive club), ANWB (Dutch motoring association), and TCS (Swiss equivalent). As the dominant digital booking and discovery platform for campings across Europe, they digitize the camping industry end-to-end — from search and booking to reviews and mapping. With 80+ international digital experts in Berlin, PiNCAMP combines the stability of ADAC backing with a startup-style product culture. Engineering is cross-functional, and the team makes real architectural decisions.

- **Funding/Stage:** Corporate-backed (ADAC, ANWB, TCS) — stable, not VC-pressured
- **Team size:** 80+ across the org; engineering team embedded in cross-functional squads
- **Engineering culture:** Autonomous squads, code reviews, architectural ownership, CI/CD, automated testing

## The Role (Job Description)
You'll be a core contributor to PiNCAMP's web engineering, building the high-traffic camping discovery and booking platform used by millions of campers across Europe.

**Responsibilities:**
- Build complex UI features using React and Next.js, implementing component-based systems
- Develop and maintain Apollo GraphQL backend-for-frontend (BFF) services
- Integrate internal and external APIs; manage authentication, caching, and schema evolution
- Optimize rendering, loading times, and data fetching for performance at scale
- Implement automated testing strategies and continuously improve CI/CD pipelines
- Conduct code reviews and mentor other developers
- Contribute to architectural decisions and engineering standards across the team
- Collaborate in cross-functional squads (product, design, engineering)

## The Ideal Candidate
**Required:**
- 5+ years in modern web engineering with proven delivery at scale
- Bachelor's/Master's in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
- Advanced React and Next.js skills — deep understanding of SSR, SSG, RSC, and hooks
- GraphQL expertise, preferably in the Apollo GraphQL ecosystem
- Strong cross-functional collaboration and communication

**Tech Stack:**
React, Next.js, Apollo GraphQL, TypeScript, Python (optional backend support), CI/CD pipelines

**Soft Skills:**

- Collaborative communicator in multi-national teams
