## The Role (Job Description)

As a Senior Fullstack Engineer at Kertos, you will:

- Build end-to-end product experiences that reduce compliance audit prep from weeks to hours
- Develop across the full stack: TypeScript/React frontend and Python/Django/FastAPI backend
- Improve automation reliability and AI-powered system recommendations (LangChain, LangGraph)
- Drive features from concept to production with measurable impact on customer outcomes
- Shape engineering practices: testing strategies, tooling improvements, code quality standards
- Deploy and maintain infrastructure on AWS using Kubernetes, Terraform, and ArgoCD
- Collaborate with product, design, and AI research teams in a cross-functional setup
